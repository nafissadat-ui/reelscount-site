Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026
ReelsCount is designed to help users understand their short-form video viewing habits and manage their time more intentionally.

Information ReelsCount Processes

ReelsCount may process information required to provide its core functionality, including:

  • Number of short-form videos watched
  • Time spent watching supported short-form video feeds
  • Daily and historical usage statistics
  • User preferences and extension settings
  • Break reminder preferences

Local Storage

ReelsCount stores usage statistics, history and preferences locally in the user's browser to provide its tracking, history and reminder features.

ReelsCount does not require users to create an account.

Personal Information

ReelsCount does not intentionally collect personal information such as names, email addresses, passwords, payment information, private messages or the content of private communications.

Browsing Activity

ReelsCount interacts with supported websites only when necessary to detect short-form video viewing activity and provide the extension's features.

The extension is designed to identify relevant short-form video activity such as viewing sessions, video counts and watch time.

Supported Platforms

ReelsCount currently supports:

  • YouTube Shorts
  • Instagram Reels
  • Facebook Reels
  • TikTok

Data Sharing and Selling

ReelsCount does not sell or rent users' personal information or browsing activity to third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by ReelsCount remains in the user's browser until it is removed through the extension, browser storage is cleared, or the extension is uninstalled.

Third-Party Platforms

ReelsCount is an independent browser extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Google, YouTube, Meta, Facebook, Instagram or TikTok.

Users should also review the privacy policies of the websites and services they use.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ReelsCount's features, functionality or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
